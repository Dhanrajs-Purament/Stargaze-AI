# StarGaze AI — Children's Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-07

> Review-ready template. The treatment of children's data is legally sensitive (COPPA in the US,
> GDPR-K in the EU, India DPDP Act 2023). Have counsel confirm your audience classification before
> release.

## Intended audience

StarGaze AI is intended for users **aged 13 and older**. It is **not** currently published under
Google Play's "Designed for Families" program and is not directed at children under 13.

## What this means in the app

- **No account, no login** — we do not ask children (or anyone) to create an account.
- **Cloud AI is off by default.** No question text leaves the device unless an adult explicitly
  enables cloud AI. The app is fully usable with on-device-only AI, so a child can use it without any
  data egress.
- **Location stays on-device** for sky computation and is not transmitted by default.
- **No behavioural advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.**

## If you operate a Kids/education edition

If you later target under-13 users or schools, you must:
- Enrol in **Designed for Families** and meet its content and SDK requirements.
- Obtain **verifiable parental consent** before any personal-data collection (COPPA).
- Under **India's DPDP Act**, obtain verifiable parental consent for minors and **not** undertake
  behavioural tracking or targeted advertising directed at children.
- Disable the cloud AI tier for child accounts (keep on-device only), and remove any data-egress paths.

This is a product decision that materially changes the AI architecture; engineering has built the app
so the cloud tier can be hard-disabled for a Kids edition.

## Parental requests

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the app, contact
**privacy@hacklet.in** and we will delete it promptly.
